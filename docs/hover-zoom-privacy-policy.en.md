# Privacy Policy — Hover Image Zoom

Last updated: October 22, 2026 (applies to version 1.0.0)
URL: https://github.com/ai2chiuo/x-media-precheck/blob/main/docs/hover-zoom-privacy-policy.en.md

## Summary
This extension shows a larger preview when you hover an image on a web page. **We have no server of our own. We collect, upload and sell no data, and use no analytics, ads or tracking.**

## What the extension reads
- On the page you are viewing, only the URL of the image under your mouse (src, srcset, data-src, a link to an image file, or a CSS background image), rewritten by a few site rules to the larger public image URL.
- The larger image is loaded by your browser directly from the original site, just as if you opened the image yourself. The extension makes no other network requests.
- It does not read forms, passwords, page text or browsing history, and does not log which images you viewed.

## Stored locally (chrome.storage.local)
- Whether zoom is enabled (on/off).
- Your list of disabled sites (domains you typed).
These settings are never synced or sent anywhere.

## What we don't do
- Sell, rent or share any data.
- Download or execute remote code.

## Deleting data
Removing the extension deletes all local settings.
